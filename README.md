Entry for the [Alien Field Notes: The Two Day Earth Review](https://make.fun/bounty/u-swift-raven-16-alien-field-notes-the-two-day-earth-7647b000) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun/r/0ene8b3q): one original line of plain text, at most 200 characters, a review of planet Earth left by an alien tourist.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the review to `/output/entry.txt`. The program refuses to write anything outside 1–200 characters.

## The line

> Lovely planet. The locals feed their dogs better than their neighbours, worship small glowing rectangles, and queue politely for coffee. 5 stars — would invade again, gently.
